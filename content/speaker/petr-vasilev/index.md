Petr Vasilev is forbundssekretær (union secretary) of EL og IT Forbundet, the Norwegian trade union for professionals in IT, electrical work and energy, representing more than 45 000 members. With a background as an IT consultant and solution architect, Petr has seen the industry from both sides of the table - building systems, delivering them as consultant and being on the customers side.
In his union role, Petr works at the intersection of technology, work life and political life. He is a prominent voice in the Norwegian debate on how AI and automation are reshaping the IT labour market. He has stood up for workers' rights in a market increasingly driven by short term contracts and consultancies, and led the union's critique of government proposals to expand police access to IP data, arguing that privacy and freedom of expression in the workplace must weigh heavier than security concerns.
Petr is a frequent commentator in Norwegian tech media and works to make the IT industry more just and inclusive. Expect a perspective Booster audiences rarely hear: not just how we build software, but on what terms.
