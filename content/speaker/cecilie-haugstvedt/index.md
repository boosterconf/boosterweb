Cecilie is a Test Lead at Systek with a technical background. She’s been working with testing for more than a decade and likes to share her ideas at conferences.

Cecilie believes the quality of the system is the responsibility of the entire team and not something you leave to testers alone. She loves working in agile teams, is a champion of automating manual processes, and has a special interest in making applications accessible for all.
