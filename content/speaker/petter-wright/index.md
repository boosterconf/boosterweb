Petter has spent over 10 years working on big and small apps including Ruter, BankID, Posten, Bulder Bank and Æ. For the past few years he has worked at Tet Digital, the technology company owned by public transport companies like Ruter and Skyss, helping scale the Ruter app to other regions in Norway. Having worked as a web developer, iOS developer, tech lead and product lead, he has seen app development from many angles and knows that great apps take more than great code.
