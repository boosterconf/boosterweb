Cathrine holds a PhD in psychology from London, but instead of staying in academia, she chose to step into the world of technology.

Today, she leads Webstep Bergen, heading a division of nearly 90 senior technology consultants. Every day, she works at the intersection of people and tech, asking a simple but powerful question, how do we help highly skilled professionals perform at their best?

Cathrine is deeply engaged in how AI and emerging technologies are reshaping not only products and business models, but leadership itself. She is particularly curious about what happens to trust, decision-making and feedback culture when technology evolves faster than organisations do.

Alongside her leadership role, she serves on corporate boards and is a committee member of TENK Tech Camp, where she is passionate about inspiring more girls to explore technology and consider careers in tech. She strongly believes that the future of technology must be built by diverse minds.

Cathrine frequently speaks about leadership, feedback culture and the human side of digital transformation, because in her view, technology is never just about code, It is about people.
