Carl likes to work across the entire process: shaping solution architecture, creating software, leading teams, working with Scrum and getting solutions into production. Most of that work happens in the cloud, primarily on Microsoft Azure. Over the past few years, he has also been an early adopter of AI-driven development, exploring how AI changes the way software is designed, built and delivered.
