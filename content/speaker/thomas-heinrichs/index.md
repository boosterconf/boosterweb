Thomas is Solution Architecture Lead at Miragon. For over eight years he has been helping organizations digitize their processes in a structured way, automate them with purpose, and transform them sustainably.

His experience spans classical process management, methodical training in BPM, and the architecture of complex automation solutions in large organizations. 
As a Solution Architect he designs scalable process architectures, integrates heterogeneous system landscapes, and ensures that functional and non-functional requirements receive the same attention as governance, compliance, and operations. 

He regularly shares his practical insights at conferences and industry events.
