title: "Chief Product Officer, Vitec Fixit Systemer AS"
---
Valentina is the Chief Product Officer at Vitec Fixit Systemer AS, where she leads the development and execution of the company’s product strategy, ensuring alignment between user needs, business objectives, and innovation. With a PhD in learning technologies, Valentina brings both a pragmatic and creative approach to solving complex challenges. With over a decade of global industry experience, she loves driving product vision, managing cross-functional teams, and leveraging data and user insights to inform strategic decisions.
