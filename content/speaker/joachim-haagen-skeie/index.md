Joachim Haagen Skeie is a senior consultant and tech lead at jPro with over 20 years of full-stack experience. He wrote Ember.js in Action (Manning), founded the EmberFest conference, and now builds his own projects with Svelte and SvelteKit.

He has spent much of his career teaching programming, including designing the curriculum for Oslo’s “Koding i Skolen” program, which reached 12,000 fourth-graders. He also runs a free makerspace for kids in Lillestrøm.

In his day job, he builds infrastructure for secure agentic coding at the Norwegian Tax Administration. So when he asks you to leave AI at the door, it’s not because he doesn’t like it. 

It’s because he knows what it can’t do for you.
