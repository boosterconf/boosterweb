Alina Deng is a product design leader based in New York with over 10 years across UX, UI, and product strategy in global tech companies and startups. She is currently Product Design Lead at Reach Financial and previously led design at OnePlus/OPPO and Acquco. She founded the Evidence-Based Design theory.
