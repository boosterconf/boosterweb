Dmitrijs started his career as a software engineer over 20 years ago and has since evolved into a Solution and Enterprise Architect. After delivering numerous software projects and learning from both successes and failures, his recent focus has shifted toward the practical integration of artificial intelligence into secure, distributed systems.

Today, Dmitry architects solutions at the intersection of AI and traditional engineering—ranging from local Edge AI implementations and autonomous hardware nodes to Zero Trust architectures and Model Context Protocol (MCP) gateways. He believes that the rush toward centralized, cloud-only AI requires a pragmatic counterbalance. His ultimate goal is to advocate for software that is built pragmatically, prioritizing data privacy, engineering rigor, and mutual respect among all stakeholders.
