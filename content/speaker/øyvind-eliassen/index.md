Øyvind Eliassen is UX Lead at Vitec Fixit in Bergen, where he is responsible for UX strategy and the design system. He has over 20 years of design experience, including UX leadership roles at Vimond, StormGeo and Wolftech, where he designed for broadcasters such as the BBC, CNN and NHK. His work covers user research, product design, prototyping and user testing.
