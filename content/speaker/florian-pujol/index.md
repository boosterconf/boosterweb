Florian Pujol is Cloud & AI Architect at Odfjell, with over 25 years of international experience in software architecture, data platforms, and AI governance. At Odfjell, he leads enterprise data platform and AI strategy.

He architected the Nexus initiative, a ground-up migration from a 10-year on-premises SQL Server data warehouse to Microsoft Fabric, and designed the governed AI-first development methodology that drives delivery velocity for both data platform and AI agent initiatives.

He has presented at Make Data Smart Bergen on Shift Left principles (2024) and "The Agentic AI Revolution" (2025).
