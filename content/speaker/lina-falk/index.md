Lina Falk (NO) is a service designer at Helse Vest IKT in Bergen, Norway. She believes design has the role and power to articulate invisible structures, listen for subtle stories and ultimately expand our collective imagination of what is possible. At HVIKT she works with patients, next of kin and health professionals to support and improve their experiences with the healthcare system.
