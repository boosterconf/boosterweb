I design learning spaces and facilitate collaborative processes that help people make better sense of what is happening, connect perspectives that rarely meet, decide what is worth trying next, and learn from what follows.

I founded F.EKS. in Bergen as the Norwegian branch of BSPW, which I co-founded in Berlin in 2018. Both names mean “for example.” They remind me that I work with organisations that are pioneering new ways of working as well as that change rarely begins with a finished blueprint. More often, it starts by paying attention to what is already happening.

For the past fifteen years, I have worked with start-ups, growing companies, established organisations, public institutions, and non-profits. Across these contexts, I have become particularly interested in the conversations, relationships, assumptions and constraints that quietly shape what becomes possible.

Based in Bergen, I work with organisations and networks across Europe. In Bergen, this has included organisations such as TV 2, Varmere Våtere Villere, BIFF and Fana Folkehøgskule.

I also write about small everyday moments that keep teaching me something about how we learn, collaborate, make sense and live together.

My academic background is in Philosophy and Art History at Humboldt University Berlin. For many years, I taught Critical Thinking at the University of Passau. I later trained as a Systemic Organisational Consultant and became a certified INQA Coach through the German Federal Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs.
