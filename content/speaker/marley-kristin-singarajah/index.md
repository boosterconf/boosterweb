Marley K Singarajah is a technology leader, speaker and co-founder of Gnist Consulting. Throughout her career, she has worked with digital product development in complex organizations, challenging silos and bringing product, design and technology closer together. She is fascinated by what makes real change happen, not only within product teams, but across organizations and society.
Today, she works at the intersection of technology, product, leadership and organizational change, with a growing focus on complex societal challenges. Through her engagement with Engineers Without Borders Norway, she also explores how technology and human leadership can contribute to problems that no single discipline or organization can solve alone.
