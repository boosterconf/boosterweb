Snorre learned to read sheet music long before learning to read code. Classical piano as a child led to a year playing keyboards in a culture school band. Code came later, and since then he has worked across most of the stack, from managing servers and cloud infrastructure to building backend services and web frontends. He currently works as a developer at REN.

His piano days are long gone, and these days his love of music has turned into a habit of collecting, and neglecting, synthesizers. Galdr is what happens when a full-stack developer gets curious about the synth in the corner.
