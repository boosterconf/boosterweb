Anshika has 14 years experience developing software at Posten Bring - Norwegian National Postal Service, building logistics systems and APIs that enable millions of people in the Nordics to order and receive packages. She values studying users, and fostering cross-departmental collaboration and building great products with her team. A typical day at work involves climbing stairs to the 18th floor of Posten Bring's towering headquarters in the heart of Oslo, where she will transform whiteboard scribbles into deployed features. 

She loves sharing fun stories and experiences at tech conferences and embracing new challenges. She's spoken at 14 international conferences over the past year including Javazone,  JFokus,  Adacon,  JCON, NDC Oslo, KotlinConf, JavaDay Istanbul, DevBcn, Devoxx Morocco, Devoxx Poland, TDC, Hello Stavanger and BoosterConf among others.

Off-duty you'll find her chasing sunsets and culinary adventures, sampling flavours from around the globe.
