Jonas wants to lease a bike. Weeks later he is still waiting, because sales, the back office and procurement each have a different picture of how his application should work. 
Sound familiar? 
Most teams debate microservices or start prompting an AI long before they agree on what the system should actually do.

In this hands-on workshop, we take Jonas's case from a fuzzy idea to decisions your team can defend:
- Align on the process with the collaborative modelling method t.bpm
- Derive capabilities and components with C4
- Decide what to build, buy or adapt with a Wardley Map
- Choose an architecture style based on the qualities that really matter

AI can challenge your thinking and speed up what comes next. But agreeing with your stakeholders on what to build and why is still your job, and it's the foundation that makes fast iteration with AI agents possible.
