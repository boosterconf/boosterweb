Designers are trained to make good decisions. But making a good decision and getting others to believe in it are two very different things.

So what gives a design decision weight?

In this talk, I'll explore evidence as the currency of design influence: how data, user behavior, research, and experimentation can turn design from an opinion into an argument people can act on.

Through a real world fintech case study, I'll show how seemingly conflicting signals challenged the problem we thought we were solving and led us toward a very different solution.
