Implementing FizzBuzz was once the hallmark of coding challenges. It is cited to be used in various job interviews to separate the wheat from the chaff.
In the age of AI-driven development, this vestige has lost a lot of its appeal.

In this workshop we will take back our humanity and explore the ancient FizzBuzz problem anew!
In the style of Oulipo, the literary ensemble that challenged themselves under artificial writing constraints, we will explore creativity in coding and what it means to be human.

Can you write code without an if? Can you solve the problem with only 80 characters? Or without using the letter "e"?

These and other constraints will serve as a fertile ground from which interesting and distinctly human code can sprout.

Bring your favorite IDE, buckle up and let the creative coding juices flow
