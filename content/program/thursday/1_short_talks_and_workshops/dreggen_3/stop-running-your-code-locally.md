Software development has become an increasing security risk. Externally, compromised packages are on the rise. Locally, AI agents can steal private data, take "inspiration" from your other projects, or accidentally format your hard drive.

We need better protection for ourselves and our development environments, and dev containers and sandboxes are a great place to start!

However, one size does not fit all. Every setup trades off three things: how secure it really is, how much it complicates the developer experience, and how many hours it takes to configure.

Join this workshop to learn different ways to isolate your code and agents, with hands-on sessions on setting up and managing worktrees and dev containers. We'll start with an overview of common dev container patterns and their trade-offs. Then comes a practical session where you can play around with some provided example projects and learn to containerize them, or perhaps containerize one of your own.

Relevant to every developer, whether or not you use AI agents.

Bring your laptop, and I'll bring the code. It should™ work on any machine. Setup instructions for what to install beforehand will be shared ahead of the workshop.
