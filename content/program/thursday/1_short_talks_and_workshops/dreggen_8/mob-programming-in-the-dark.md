I have hosted several "Coding in the Dark" competitions, and the response was always unanimous: "This was so much fun! Let's do this again!"

Coding in the Dark is a front-end coding competition. Contestants get a screenshot of a website or svg and 15 minutes to rebuild it in HTML and CSS, without ever previewing their result. When time runs out, the results are revealed and the audience votes for the winner.

Turns out it is even more fun in groups!

"Mob Programming in the Dark" works the same way, but in teams. Only the programmer works blind. Their teammates can watch the spectator screen, which shows every team's result live, and they guide the programmer toward the target.

This event is open for all, are you a programmer who wants to see if you can still remember building basic HTML and CSS in the age of AI? Or have you never written a line of code, but want to help out by explaining to your teammates what to do to get closer to the winning result? There's a seat on the team for everyone.

Join us for a fun little game of coding and communication!
