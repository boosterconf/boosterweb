Visualizing sound, or more specifically music, can really enhance the experience. Tailored visualizations have been around for a long time and have been a staple of live concerts and other forms of live music for a long time. Remember WinAmp? Or maybe good ol' Windows Media Player? Looking into a pulsing swirling vortex of spaced out colors while your favorite track blasted in your ears.

How did they do that? How can you do that? By looking at music like data, and applying some visualization to it! We'll look at how we can take sound apart and look at the raw data underneath. We'll look at how we can narrow in on specific features of a piece of music and use that in a visual way. All in the browser of course, with Web Audio and HTMLCanvas.

Familiarity with JavaScript and browsers will be helpful. Bring a laptop!
