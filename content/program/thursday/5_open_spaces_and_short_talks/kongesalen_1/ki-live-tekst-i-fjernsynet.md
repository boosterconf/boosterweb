TV 2 Norway is using and developing AI for subtitles of live transmissions on the linear channels, and are aiming to implement this on live transmission on the streaming plattform TV 2 Play. 
Live subtitling may read like an easy task, but has a set of technical challenges related to live events. Not to mention the user experience.
