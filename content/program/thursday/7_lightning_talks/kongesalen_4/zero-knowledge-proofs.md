Let's say you are in a finding Waldo competition and exclaim "I found him!". Your friends, also in the competition and in it to win it, demands a proof.
How can you convince your friends that you know where Waldo is, without revealing anything else, not even his location?

In this lightning talk you will learn about zero knowledge proof, will be given some examples and how they can be used.

You will walk away with a deeper understanding of fascinating and counter-intuitive notion of zero knowledge proofs.
