There are few Linux distributions that can measure up against the "reproducability" of NixOS. Sadly not all server providers have NixOS support.

But we do not need support! We will discuss novel ways of installing NixOS on different VPS providers. Show a little of how it works under the hood (hint: it is a kernel feature). And the powers this grants by using the Nix language to configure servers.
