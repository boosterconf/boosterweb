When distributed systems meet safety-critical infrastructure.

In most software systems, a delayed message, a failed request, or an automatic retry is just another Tuesday. But what happens when those messages control road tunnels, ventilation systems, traffic signals, and other critical infrastructure?

In this talk, we’ll explore how familiar distributed systems concepts change when software interacts with the physical world, and why a perfectly reasonable software design can lead to dangerous consequences.
