You’re trying to log into a site and you get the familiar prompt, «please enter the code we just texted you», in this presentation I’ll talk about why you shouldn’t use this method of logging in, and what better alternatives already exist today!

You’ll see a story about how I SIM-swapped my boss during my first month at a new job, and how to prevent such things from happening to you :)
