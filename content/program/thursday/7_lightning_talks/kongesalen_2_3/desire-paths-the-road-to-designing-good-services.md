What if we design solutions based on what people actually do instead of what we assume or want them to? That is the goal of service design, a human-centered approach that factors in the entire journey, experience and ecosystem of a service. By including designers from the start, service design paves around existing behavior, avoids expensive detours and solves the right problem instead of decorating the wrong solution.
