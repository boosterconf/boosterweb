Join this workshop to learn how to simulate, debug and handle network errors!

Participants will learn about dropped packets, stale connections, timeouts, TCP Keep-Alive and retries. There will be exercises that introduce the participants to Linux tools such as `ss`, `tcpdump`, `strace`, `ebpftrace` and `nftables`.

Prerequisites: A laptop capable of running Linux VMs.

To ensure a smooth setup, it is advised to go through the setup guide in advance.

Setup and exercise details at https://github.com/ivarref/boosterconf2027
