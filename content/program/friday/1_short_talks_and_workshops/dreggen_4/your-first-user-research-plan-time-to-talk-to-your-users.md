You have users, but you don’t really talk to them. (But you kind of want to?)

Good news: this is the workshop where you finally sit down, get help, and do it!

Kim, an experienced UX researcher, will guide you step by step through setting up your own research, so you can run it yourself. Together we’ll go from a vague “we should really talk to our users” to a real plan: a sharp research question, the right people to invite, a method that fits your team, and a recruitment plan with a screener. You get all the tips and tricks, plus the tools and links she uses. And you learn by doing, not by listening to slides.

This workshop is for everyone who finally wants to talk to their users. No research experience needed, just curiosity about why you’re doing it. Come alone or with a colleague, and bring a real question about your product.* In 90 minutes, we’ll dive into how you can find out what your users really need on your topic. Maybe you’ll finally replace that stakeholder discussion full of assumptions with real insights.

You’ll leave with a research proposal that’s mostly filled in and a plan you can run next week. (OK, maybe in 2 weeks, because recruitment takes time. That’s one of the things you’ll learn here.)

You’ll also get a feel for what good research takes, so you know where you can go it alone and where it’s worth calling in an expert.

Let’s finally do this!

*No need for a perfectly ironed-out question! If you don’t have one yet, Kim will bring a few topics that are always worth exploring. You do need a product with users (or people you could realistically reach), so you have someone to learn from.
