Join me for a hands-on workshop where we use C++ to turn a Daisy Pod development board into a small, playable hardware synthesizer.

Participants will program the board, connect headphones directly to it, and use its physical knobs and buttons to control the synthesizer. Throughout the workshop we will explore some of the fundamental concepts behind digital synthesis and real-time audio programming, with a focus on learning by building and experimenting.

No previous experience with synthesizers, DSP or embedded programming is required .

Prerequisites:
 - A laptop
 - Wired headphones/earbuds if possible.

The Daisy Pod development board will be provided during the workshop.
