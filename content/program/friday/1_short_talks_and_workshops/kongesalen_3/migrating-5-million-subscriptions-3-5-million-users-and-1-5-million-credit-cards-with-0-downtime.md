TV 2 has invested a lot in its TV 2 Play streaming platform and currently has several million active subscriptions at any given time, through both direct and partnered channels. While this has primarily been a successful story of fantastic growth, TV 2 still needed to replace their old and monolithic order and billing systems underpinning this solution. The existing platform was showing its age, and had several limitations that hindered innovation and made delivery of important business requirements significantly more complicated and slow - or in some cases not feasible at all. 

In our presentation we will tell the story of how we broke up a single on-prem platform into logical smaller services in the cloud, and how we seamlessly migrated more than 3 million users - on-the-fly and while the service was continuously running - use case by use case: how to give correct access to content for users, card payments, active subscriptions, product configuration, e-mail handling, stream kicking, and more. You will learn about approaches that worked and some that didn’t, and lessons learned from a successful migration project that nonetheless took around 2 years to complete. The talk will be quite technical with specific examples of technologies and strategies used.
