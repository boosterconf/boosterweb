Have you ever needed to stop a release of your microservice and wait until other parts of the system got released first? Have you ever needed to roll back to the previous version because your service's API wasn't compatible with its consumers? Have you ever experienced code freezes?

Chances are, you have experienced some of those, but there is good news: in most situations, you can avoid these issues and achieve truly independent deployments and releases.

Many patterns and strategies can help you untangle dependencies between services and make your team's life much easier and less stressful.

During this talk, you'll learn about API Evolution Patterns, Database Migrations, and the fundamentals of introducing changes to APIs and databases in a backward and forward compatible way. I'll show you how to prevent broken integrations with other services and frontend applications, along with practical techniques and tools that can help you avoid these issues.

I look forward to seeing you at my talk!
