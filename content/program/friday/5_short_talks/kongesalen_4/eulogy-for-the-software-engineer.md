Somebody should say a few words.

For two years this profession has been mourning itself in fragments: a layoff post here, a résumé quietly updated there, a product demo watched with the sound off and a knot in the gut. Nobody has stood up and made it official. This talk is a funeral service, with three objects from my desk to be laid to rest and a headstone that turns out to have the wrong name on it.

I've been shipping software for almost twenty years with no degree, a lot of forum threads, and two exits. I've spent this year merging thousands of lines I didn't type and asking what, exactly, I'm still for. My answer is uncomfortable and hopeful in equal measure: the part of the job that got automated was the part nobody owned, and the part that made you worth hiring never left your head. The catch is that it used to grow on its own, and it doesn't anymore.

Come mourn. Leave with a renewed job description, one assignment for the week, and a reason to keep handing what you know to somebody else.
