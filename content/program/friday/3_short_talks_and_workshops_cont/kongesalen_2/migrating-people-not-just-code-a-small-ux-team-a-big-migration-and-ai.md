Moving a legacy system to the cloud is usually told as a technical story about databases and APIs. For the people who use the software every day, it's a different story: trusted workflows disappear, muscle memory breaks, and the unwritten rules they have relied on for years stop working. 

At Vitec Fixit we are moving our salon management platform from a desktop installation to the cloud, while hundreds of salons keep running their business on it every day. The architecture turned out to be the “easy” part. The hard part was everything around it: a scope nobody could fully define, a company that didn't share one picture of what “done” meant, very little UX capacity, and business rules that existed only in how our users worked around the old system. 

This is the story of how we tried to do proper UX under those conditions, and how AI became our way to stretch a small UX team.  

We will share: 

how we built a shared understanding of the scope when the documentation, the people and the opinions all disagreed 

how a small UX team used AI to turn years of scattered evidence into something the whole company could act on 

where AI got our users wrong, and how we caught it before it reached the product 

For product managers, UX people, and tech teams facing a legacy migration with fewer resources than they would like.
