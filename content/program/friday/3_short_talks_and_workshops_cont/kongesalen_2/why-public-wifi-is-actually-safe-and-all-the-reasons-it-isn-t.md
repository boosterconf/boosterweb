Your security team might have you believe that public WiFi is the devil and must be burned at the stake, its MAC address purged from the earth and its very existence condemned to the eternal pits of hell.

And, to be fair, they're not entirely wrong.

This talk explores what actually happens when you connect to an untrusted network:

What attackers can see, what they can manipulate, which attacks remain practical and which ones are relics of a less encrypted and older internet.

We will explore genuine risks from security paranoia and ask whether public WiFi really deserves its reputation or if we've been carrying deprecated ideas.
