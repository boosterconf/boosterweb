AI is supposed to be a massive disruptor in how we create apps. Yet, large apps don't change as quickly as you'd expect and the new wave of vibe-coded apps hasn't shaken up the app market as much as many predicted.

As tech lead and acting product lead on the Ruter and Skyss apps, used by 1.5 million people, I've seen first-hand where the real bottlenecks are. Spoiler: it isn't the code.

AI can do a lot of things fast, but not necessarily the things that matter. You still need to figure out where you can YOLO and where your decisions need real care. Small decisions can have a massive impact at scale, while work that takes months in a large organization sometimes barely matters. And when a developer can build ten features in a day, someone needs to know when to hit the brakes.

In this talk I'll share lessons from both the large apps I've worked on and my hobby project, FestivalGuide. I'll talk about why shipping matters, why good data matters and why design principles coined in the 70s matter more than ever before.
