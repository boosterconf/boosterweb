Have you ever examined a code base and wondered what thought process led to the sprawling mess before you? Or, have you ordered a software solution and been horrified by the low quality of what you got?

Who designed this?!

Usually, nobody did.

The journey from business problem to code is long and perilous, but we're so focused on the destination that we don't notice when we veer off course.

Instead of proposing yet another coding principle, I'll share a perspective I find useful for keeping projects on course from business problem to code, one you can use whether you're a developer or an invested client.
