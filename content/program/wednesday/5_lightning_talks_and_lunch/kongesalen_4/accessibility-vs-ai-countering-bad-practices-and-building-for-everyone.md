As AI is taking over not only how we build for the web, but also what we build and for whom, we need to make sure that we leave no one behind. Does an AI trained on millions of websites full of accessibility barriers just multiply the problems, or does it learn from our mistakes and good resources on how to do better? That is up to us! Come learn what steps you can take to rein in the AI and make sure what we build can be used by everyone.
