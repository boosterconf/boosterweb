We all love a good success story, but sometimes the most interesting stories are the spectacular failures. The CueCat raised hundreds of millions to put a barcode scanner on your desk, Google Wave tried to reinvent communication, and Juicero raised $120 million to build a machine that squeezed juice bags.

So how do smart people, great technology and enormous amounts of money end up producing something nobody wants?

Join me as we dive into some of tech’s most infamous failures and ask what we can learn from them. The goal isn’t to laugh at people who got it wrong. It’s to "steal" their lessons before we make the same mistakes ourselves.

Because failure is expensive. Learning from somebody else’s failure, is free!
