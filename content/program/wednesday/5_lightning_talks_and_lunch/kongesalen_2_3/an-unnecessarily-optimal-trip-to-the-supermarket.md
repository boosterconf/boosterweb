Going to the supermarket is not a particularly difficult problem.

But what happens if we stop relying on common sense and instead approach grocery shopping as a mathematical optimization problem?

What starts as a simple shopping trip quickly turns into a surprisingly elaborate exercise in modelling everyday decisions.

A story about optimization, overengineering, and the pursuit of the optimal grocery trip.
