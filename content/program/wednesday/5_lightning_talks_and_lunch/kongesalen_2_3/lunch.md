Is your lunch break the most boring half hour of the day? 
Have you run out of small talk with the same five colleagues? 
Would you occasionally like to betray your boss and get away with it?

At Zrch we replaced the awkward silence with a deck of cards. 
What started as one person bringing a game to the table turned into a daily ritual that changed how our team talks to each other - including the parts of the teams that never talked before.

This lightning talk is the short version of what we learned: 
how to pick games that survive a 30-minute lunch break, 
how to turn a one-off into a habit that outlives its founder, 
and what nobody tells you about the downsides. 
You will leave with four concrete games you can bring to your own office tomorrow, 
and a rough idea of how to get people to actually sit down and play them.

Rather than walking through rulebooks in detail on stage, 
we have the games with us: they will be available throughout the conference, 
and we will happily teach them during the breaks. 
Come find us and play a round.

No prior gaming experience required. Bringing snacks is encouraged.

---------

Takeaways:
- A set of criteria for choosing games that fit a lunch break
- Four field-tested games, with the reasons each one works
- Practical tactics for starting and sustaining the habit
- An honest look at where lunch-time gaming goes wrong
