Most employers these days offer great quality chairs, desks, and expensive mechanical keyboards, but many of us still struggle with arm, neck, and shoulder pain on a daily basis. 

What have we overlooked, and what can we do about it ?

This talk is about my experience of years of pain and suffering, what I have learned from research, physical therapists, surgeons,/ doctors,  and how I finally overcame my daily pain.
