We call it a leadership team. But do we just meet to report status, or do we actually solve things together?

Every leader has their own area, their own KPIs and their own people. The meetings are status updates, and then everyone goes back to their own. Sound familiar?

A leadership team solves things together. A group of leaders informs each other. Many are in the latter category, and the potential of the group never gets used.

In this lightning talk you get a quick test of whether your group is a leadership team, and one thing you can try at your next management meeting.
