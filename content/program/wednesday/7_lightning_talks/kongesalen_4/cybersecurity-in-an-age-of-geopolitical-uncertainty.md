Nothing changes in the IT department overnight, however, recent geopolitical changes have significantly changed who we trust. We depend and trust large cloud providers, political changes leaves us questioning if we can trust them and with what. We need to move our thinking from assets and vulnerabilities to geopolitical threats, dependencies and consequences. This talk introduces you to a different mindset.
