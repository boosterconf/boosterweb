It is often said that you cannot manage what you cannot measure.
But what happens when measurements are missing, or when installing measurement instruments is impossible?
This is one of the challenges facing the oil and gas industry, where the installations are old while our regulations and emissions awareness evolve. 
In this lightning talk, I will share insights from FlareVision, an Equinor project, where we combine video streams, sensor data, and computer vision to improve our measurements and emissions reporting from offshore flaring activities.
