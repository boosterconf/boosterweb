A developer in a new role, in a new organisation, with zero project management training. What could possibly go wrong?

This talk is about the gap between developers and the business side, and what happens when nobody knows who should decide, in a field where laws and regulations keep changing. Drawing on Amy Edmondson's "The Fearless Organization", I'll share what I learned about psychological safety, what worked (and didn't) for my team, and whether stepping away from the code made me a better developer.

You'll leave with concrete advice for developers making the same leap, and practical tools to help developers, product owners and project managers actually understand each other. 

Spoiler: Jira was the easy part.
