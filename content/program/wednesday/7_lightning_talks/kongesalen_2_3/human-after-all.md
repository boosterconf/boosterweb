AI can write our code, summarise our meetings, answer our questions and increasingly act like someone who understands us. But in a world where technology can do more and more for us, what do we still need from each other?

Our brains are fundamentally social. We need other people, and not just people who agree with us, but people who challenge us, frustrate us, make us laugh and force us to see the world differently. Trust, empathy and belonging are built through these interactions.

In this 10-minute lightning talk, I’ll explore what happens when technology meets a human brain, and why our human skills may become more important, not less, as AI gets better.

I will give the audience seven small, practical things they can do to build more trust, empathy and human connection, both at work and beyond.

Because the better technology gets at being technology, the better we need to be at being human.
