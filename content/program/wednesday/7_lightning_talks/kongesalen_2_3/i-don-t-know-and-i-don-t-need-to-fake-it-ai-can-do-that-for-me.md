AI is changing how we work, how we learn, and even how we judge our own competence. When everyone suddenly has an expert in their pocket, what does it mean to actually know something? And what happens to impostor syndrome when AI makes it easier than ever to pretend that I know?
How do I stay curious enough to ask the “stupid” questions, admit when I don’t know, and challenge answers that sound convincing?
The fear of falling behind in the AI race can make us run faster without asking where we’re going. In trying to keep up, are we at risk of losing some of ourselves — our confidence, curiosity, and willingness to think for ourselves?
I’ll explore what happens when AI meets our professional insecurities, why communication and trust matter more than ever, and why saying “I don’t know” might be one of the most valuable things we can do.
