15 years heavily involved in political parties and organizations have made me a better developer. In this talk, I’ll share my insights, enabling you to utilize them yourself without spending countless hours in political meetings, talking to voters or arguing policies.

I’ll also draw some comparisons you probably haven’t thought about, and share what mistakes I see fellow developers do, that politics have taught me to avoid. And, of course, I’ll tell you about how I’ve screwed up as well.
