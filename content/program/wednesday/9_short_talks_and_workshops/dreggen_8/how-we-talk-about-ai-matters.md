I think, I lie, I make mistakes, I hallucinate. Who am I? 

If your answer is "AI," please join us in the “How we talk about AI matters“ workshop!  

We are all experiencing the sudden boom in LLMs around us. And we need a language to describe this new phenomenon. With chatbots texting like humans, management presenting agents as our new colleagues, and the human tendency to see a face on anything, it's no surprise we use anthropomorphic language when talking about AI. However, the implied illusion that LLM chatbots have human traits can be dangerous and hinder your productivity.

In this workshop, Anna and Maja will guide you through interactions where LLMs straddle the line between human and inhuman patterns of behavior. You’ll then work in groups to find ways to move beyond the anthropomorphic language we use to describe our interactions with LLMs.
