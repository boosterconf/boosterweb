We used to have a problem at Widerøe: our applications kept crashing, and we didn't know why. We decided to take a page from, well, ourselves, and established a post-mortem procedure based on the same principles that have made flying the safest form of transport there is.

In this session I will take you through how we do post-mortems at Norwegian and Widerøe and look at how this practice has helped us raise the quality bar and allows us to deploy on Friday afternoons with record-low cortisol levels.

Expect to walk away from the talk with a newfound appreciation for the importance of investigating the things that go wrong, as well as a toolkit you can use to conduct effective post-mortems.
