What happens when developers are brought into discovery and designers into durability?
Across different organizations, we’ve worked to break down the traditional boundaries between product, design and technology. We’ve brought developers closer to users, problems and discovery, while bringing designers closer to the technical realities and long-term durability of what we build.
In this experience talk, we’ll share stories from organizations where we’ve tried to make this shift happen. What did we actually change? What worked, what didn’t, and what happened when people started crossing the traditional boundaries of their roles?
We’ll share what we’ve learned about creating shared ownership across the product lifecycle, changing established ways of working, and building a culture where product, design and technology take responsibility for the outcome together.
We believe this matters more than ever in the age of AI. As AI challenges traditional role boundaries, the future of product development may depend less on protecting our disciplines and more on learning to cross them.
